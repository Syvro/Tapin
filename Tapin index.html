Connect your Arduino

Chrome or Edge can read the reader directly over USB. Have your sketch print each card’s ID on its own line:

Serial.begin(9600);
// after reading the card:
Serial.println(uidAsHex);  // e.g. A1B2C3D4

Readers that act as a keyboard also work: just tap a card while this page is open.
